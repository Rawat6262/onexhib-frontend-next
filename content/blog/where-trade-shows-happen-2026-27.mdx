If you are deciding where to exhibit next year, it helps to know where the events
actually are. This is a straightforward count of the **2,134 upcoming and ongoing
exhibitions** listed on OneXhib — no estimates, no projections, just what is on the
calendar right now.

> All figures are a snapshot of OneXhib listings on 9 September 2026 and will shift
> as organisers publish new dates.

Germany leading is no shock — Messe Frankfurt, Messe München and Koelnmesse have
anchored the European calendar for decades. Poland at number two is the genuinely
interesting entry, and the reason becomes obvious when you look at venues rather
than countries.

## One venue explains Poland's position

| Venue | Upcoming exhibitions |
| --- | --- |
| Ptak Warsaw Expo | 171 |
| Shanghai New International Expo Center | 57 |
| National Exhibition and Convention Center (NECC), Shanghai | 20 |
| Bangkok International Trade & Exhibition Centre (BITEC) | 18 |
| Messe München | 16 |
| São Paulo Expo | 15 |
| IFEMA Madrid | 14 |
| Bombay Exhibition Centre (NESCO) | 14 |

Ptak Warsaw Expo alone accounts for roughly two thirds of Poland's listings. It is
the largest exhibition centre in Central and Eastern Europe, and it runs an
unusually dense programme of sector fairs rather than a handful of giant annual
events. If you are targeting the CEE market, one venue relationship covers a lot
of ground.

## Cities: Shanghai first, then a long tail

| City | Upcoming exhibitions |

Nadarzyn is where Ptak Warsaw Expo sits, just outside Warsaw — which is why it
outranks cities many times its size. Treat it as "Warsaw metropolitan area"
rather than a city in its own right.

What stands out is how quickly the numbers fall away. After the top handful, no
single city dominates. Southeast Asia in particular is spread across Kuala Lumpur,
Bangkok and Jakarta rather than concentrated in one hub.

## What to do with this

A few practical readings:

- **If you want volume and proximity in Europe**, Germany and Poland between them
  hold 578 upcoming events — more than a quarter of everything listed.
- **If you are entering Asia**, China's 226 events are concentrated in Shanghai,
  while Southeast Asia's are spread across three countries. Those need different
  travel plans.
- **If your sector is niche**, the long tail matters more than the leaderboard.
  The [country and city pages](/exhibitions-in) are a faster way to find the two or
  three events that actually fit than scanning a global list.

Every number here comes from listings you can open and check yourself. If you spot
an event that is missing or wrong, [tell us](/contact) — the data is only as good
as what organisers publish.
